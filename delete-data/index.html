Requesting deletion of your data

Tideline · last updated 4 September 2026

Most of what Tideline holds is not held by anyone but you, so most of it needs no request at all. This page is honest about which is which.

Your check-ins — delete them yourself, right now

Every check-in, note, check-in type and setting lives in a database on your own device and is never sent to me or to anyone else. There is no account and no server copy, so there is nothing for me to delete and no request to make.

  • One entry — open it in the app and delete it. It is gone immediately.
  • Everything — uninstall Tideline. Android removes the app's data with it.

If you made a backup file, that file is wherever you put it. Delete it the way you would delete any other file; I have no copy and no way to reach it.

How to ask

Use the developer contact details published with the app on its Google Play listing. Google requires those to be current, so they are the address that reaches me.

Because there is no account, there is no name or email to look you up by. To let me find the right purchase record, include the Google Play order number from your purchase receipt — Google emails it at the time of purchase, and it also appears under Payments & subscriptions in your Google account. Without it I can delete crash reports but cannot identify which purchase record is yours.

Requests are actioned within 30 days, as the GDPR requires.

What I cannot delete

Google is the merchant for anything bought through Google Play, and Google keeps its own record of the transaction for tax and accounting reasons. That record is Google's, not mine, and is governed by Google's privacy policy — deleting my copy does not remove theirs, and I have no way to make it.
