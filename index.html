Tideline — Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

Tideline is a symptom diary. The symptoms you log are the most sensitive thing this app holds, and they never leave your device. This policy explains that in full, and is honest about the two things that do leave it.

Who is responsible

Tideline is developed and published by Zoltan Demant, an individual developer. For anything in this policy, including requests about your data, use the developer contact details published with the app on its Google Play listing. Google requires those to be current, so they are the address that reaches me.

What stays on your device

Everything you actually log is stored in a database on your device and is never transmitted to me or to anyone else. That covers:

  • Your check-ins — what you logged, the date, the 1–10 severity, and any note you wrote.
  • The check-in types you create, including their names and colors.
  • Your settings, including your reminder time and week start.

There is no account, no sign-up, and no sync. I cannot see your check-ins, and there is no server holding them.

Backups you make yourself

Tideline can save a copy of everything you have logged to a file. That file is written wherever you choose to put it, and from that point it is in your hands — if you place it in a cloud folder, it goes to that cloud provider under their terms, not mine. Restoring reads a file back in and never deletes what is already there.

The doctor's report

The report is generated on your device from your own check-ins and is shared only if and when you choose to share it. It is not uploaded anywhere.

What does leave your device

Two things, both from established providers acting on my behalf, and neither of them containing anything you have logged.

WhoWhat they receiveWhy
RevenueCat Your purchase history and an anonymous, randomly generated identifier for your installation. No name, no email, no account. To know whether you have paid for the premium report, and to restore that purchase on a new device.
Sentry Crash and freeze reports: a stack trace, your device model, your Android version, and the Tideline version. Personally identifiable information is switched off in the app's configuration, so no IP address, no device name, and nothing you have logged. To find out that the app broke, and where, so it can be fixed.

Sentry data is processed in the European Union. Both providers encrypt data in transit. Their own policies are at revenuecat.com/privacy and sentry.io/privacy.

The purchase itself is handled by Google Play. I never see your payment details — Google does not give them to me, and Tideline never asks for them.

What Tideline does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier is read or transmitted.
  • No analytics or usage tracking. I do not know which screens you open or how often you log.
  • No selling or renting of data, to anyone, ever.
  • No location access, no contacts, no photos, no microphone.
  • No third-party sign-in, and no social features.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet — to check your purchase and to send a crash report.
  • Notifications — to show the daily reminder, if you switch it on.
  • Run at startup — so a reminder you have set survives a restart.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data held about you, and you can object to processing. In practice this concerns only the purchase record and any crash reports, since nothing else reaches me. Write to me using the developer contact details on Tideline's Google Play listing and I will action it. You can also complain to your national data protection authority; in Sweden that is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

Your check-ins need no request: uninstalling Tideline removes them with the app, and deleting an entry in the app deletes it outright. For everything else, including what I can and cannot delete and what to include so a purchase record can be found, see Requesting deletion of your data.

Legal basis and retention

Purchase data is processed to perform the contract you enter into when you buy the premium report, and is retained while the purchase remains valid and for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Crash reports are processed under legitimate interest in keeping the app working, and are retained by Sentry for 90 days by default.

Children

Tideline is not directed at children under 13, and it collects nothing that would identify a child. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact me and I will remove it.

Not medical advice

Tideline records what you tell it. It does not diagnose, treat, or advise, and nothing in it is a substitute for a qualified clinician.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
